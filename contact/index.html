Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions students ask us most.

Who can join IEEE CSTMC?

Any TMU student is welcome at our events, whatever your program or year. Most of our members study engineering or computer science, but you don't have to.

Do events cost anything?

Most of our events are free for TMU students. If an event has a fee or limited spots, we'll say so on the sign-up page.

Do I need any experience?

No. Our workshops are built for beginners and walk you through everything step by step. If you already know your stuff, come anyway: it's a great way to meet people and help others.

How do I hear about upcoming events?

Follow @ieeecstmc on Instagram and join our mailing list. Every event is posted there first, and you can also check our Events page.

How can I join the executive team?

We recruit new executives and first-year representatives (FYRs) each school year. Keep an eye on our Instagram for applications, or email us to ask about open roles.

My company wants to sponsor or partner with you. Where do I start?

We'd love to hear from you. See our sponsorship tiers on the Sponsors page, or email us at ieee.comp@torontomu.ca.
