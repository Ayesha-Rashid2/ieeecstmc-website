Mission & Vision

About Us

The IEEE Computer Society Toronto Metropolitan Chapter (CSTMC), originally founded as the "Hobby Computer Club" at Ryerson University, has grown into a cornerstone for students seeking to enhance their programming knowledge, connect with like-minded peers, and network with industry professionals.

Our mission is to empower students to invest in their futures by providing opportunities and resources to develop both technical and soft skills through workshops and networking events.
